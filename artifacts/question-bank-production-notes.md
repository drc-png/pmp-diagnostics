# Assessment-bank production standard

The complete Assessment Center target is 1,750 unique questions:

- Baseline diagnostic: 50
- Five focused practice banks: 750
- Midpoint checkpoint: 50
- Five independent full-length simulations: 900

No renamed, reordered, or lightly reskinned duplicate counts as a unique question.

Each release batch must pass:

1. 2026 ECO domain and task mapping.
2. Originality review.
3. Four plausible, parallel answer choices.
4. Answer-key and answer-length balance.
5. Corpus-level repeated-phrase and cue analysis.
6. Rationale review for authority, evidence, timing, governance, and proportionality.
7. Desktop and mobile interaction tests.
8. Score calculation and incomplete-attempt tests.
9. Google Sheet submission and learner-email matching.
10. WordPress dashboard retrieval.

The five 150-question focused banks should be produced in 25-question editorial batches. A bank should remain unavailable until all six batches pass the same validation and are combined into the released bank.
