Questions before joining

Clear answers for serious candidates.

Is this aligned with the 2026 PMP® exam updates?

Yes. The cohort is built for the updated exam, including the new domain weighting, deeper scenario focus, the expanded Business Environment domain, AI, sustainability, and value delivery. You prepare for the exam PMI is moving toward.

How does PMBOK® Guide Eighth Edition show up?

PMBOK® 8 informs the material as a supporting reference. The exam itself is built on PMI’s Exam Content Outline, so that is what the cohort is structured around, with PMBOK® 8 reinforcing the concepts.

Do I need AI experience?

No. The AI for Project Managers module meets you where you are and shows practical, governance-aware ways to use AI in real project work, which is exactly the direction the exam is taking.

Is this program right for CAPM® holders?

Yes. CAPM® holders receive a bridge guide that explains what changes at PMP® level and where the exam shifts from foundational knowledge to leadership judgment.

Are payment plans available?

Yes. Reach out and we will walk you through the options so the investment fits your timeline.

Should I register now or take the diagnostic first?

Either works. If you are ready to commit to a guided plan, claim your seat. If you want to see where you stand first, take the free diagnostic. Both lead to the same place: a clear path to the credential.
